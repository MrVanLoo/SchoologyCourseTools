Nothing is uploaded: everything happens on this computer, and it works with no internet.

1Course export

How to get the export from Schoology
  1. In the course, open Course Options and choose Save Course to Resources.
  2. In Resources, open that folder's menu and choose Export, then Common Cartridge.
  3. Download the file from Transfer History. The link stops working after about a day.
